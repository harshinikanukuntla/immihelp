SponsorScope

Sponsorship signals from public government data, with resume matching that runs entirely on your own machine.

Your resume

Used to score how closely your background matches a job description. It is stored only in this browser, is never sent to our servers, and there is no account to attach it to. Removing it deletes the text and everything computed from it.

Checking…

Plain text works best. Formatting, images, and columns are ignored by the model.

What the panel shows

Lookup service

The sponsorship API is open source and stores nothing about who asks. Point this at your own deployment if you would rather not use the public one.

What leaves your device

  • Sent: the company name shown on the posting, and its country when we can tell. Nothing else.
  • Never sent: your resume, the job description, the page URL, any identifier, or any cookie. The API sets none.
  • Stored on our side: a daily count per company name, so we know which companies to prioritise. It is not joinable to a person.
